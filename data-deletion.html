Data Deletion

SPLAT: Block Puzzle — Block Puzzle Game for Android

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

SPLAT has no account. We never ask you to register, we run no servers, and we hold no copy of your game data. Your progress lives on your own device and, if you have signed in to Google Play Games, in your own Google Account — Sections 1 and 2 explain how to erase both. Advertising and analytics data is a separate matter, held by Google; Section 3 covers it.

1. Data on Your Device

Your progress, settings and any interrupted run are stored in the App's private storage. To remove them:

  • Reset your progress in the game — Settings → RESET PROGRESS. This clears your unlocked leagues, best scores and tutorial state.
  • Clear all App data — Android Settings → Apps → SPLAT → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the App. This removes everything, including your settings.

Resetting progress also asks Google to delete your cloud save. The App sends that request whenever you reset, but it is silent and not guaranteed: if you are offline or signed out of Play Games it will not happen, and your progress can come back from the cloud the next time you play. Treat RESET PROGRESS as destructive if you are signed in — and if you need certainty either way, use Section 2 below, which is the only route we can vouch for.

2. Cloud Saves and Leaderboard Scores

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your cloud save and your leaderboard scores are stored by Google under your own Google Account. Deleting them through your Google Account is the only route that is certain: we cannot reach your leaderboard scores at all, and although the App asks Google to delete the cloud save when you reset your progress, it receives no confirmation that the deletion happened.

Google's instructions are here: Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile. You can delete the data for this one game without touching your other games. Google performs the deletion and defines its own scope.

Do this in the right order, or it undoes itself. The App uploads a cloud save shortly after your progress changes and whenever it goes into the background. If you delete your Play Games data and then open the game again while signed in, the copy still on your device is uploaded and the deletion is undone. Reset your progress or uninstall the App first, then delete through your Google Account.

To stop anything further from being stored in your Google Account, sign out of Play Games on your device. That is the only setting that stops both.

Switching publication off in Settings → PUBLISH SCORES does less than it sounds like: it stops future scores being submitted to the public leaderboards, but your best scores remain part of the private cloud save, which keeps being written while you are signed in. It also does not remove scores that have already been published.

3. Advertising and Analytics Data

To reset or delete your advertising identifier, open Android's settings — on most devices under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads. To stop further analytics and advertising collection where the App asks for consent, reopen the dialog through the privacy options in the App's Settings screen and withdraw it.

Data already collected through these services sits on Google's systems and carries a randomly generated identifier rather than your name or account. We cannot find your records from your name or your email address. If you are able to supply your app-instance identifier, we can ask Google to delete the analytics events recorded against it.

4. Requesting Deletion by Email

Write to support-komandakycto@proton.me with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will delete what we hold that relates to you, except anything we are required to keep by law. We aim to answer within one month, or sooner where the law that applies to you requires it. Please do not send identity documents unless we ask for something we reasonably need in order to verify your request.
